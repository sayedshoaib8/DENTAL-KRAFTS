Tooth Preservation & Relief

Don't Ignore Tooth Pain

Persistent tooth sensitivity or throbbing pain is often a sign that the inner pulp is inflamed or infected. Seeking an early dental consultation allows our doctors to evaluate the tooth, relieve the root cause of discomfort, and preserve your natural tooth structure.

  • Lingering sensitivity to hot or cold foods and drinks
  • Sharp or dull throbbing sensation when biting or chewing
  • Gum tenderness, swelling, or localized pressure
